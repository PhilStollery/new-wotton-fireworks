TITO ACTIVITY MODEL EXPECTED BY V25
-----------------------------------
# Event Capacity          3,500 overall admissions
# Parking Capacity          150 all parking tickets
Release 1 - Super Saver     300
Release 2 - Advance         700
Release 3 - Standard        unlimited at release level

All admission types that count toward attendance, including comps/pre-school where appropriate, should also count against # Event Capacity. Standard therefore uses remaining Event Capacity rather than a fixed 2,500 website cap.

QUICK TEST
----------
1. Confirm the What's On chips are readable (dark text on bright yellow) and programme times/body copy have adequate contrast.
2. Confirm the Prices table shows Pre-school first, then Primary / Secondary & Sixth Form / Adult across Super Saver / Advance / Standard.
3. Confirm the separate Parking tickets table has only Ticket + Price columns and its Price header says “150 spaces until sold out or 7.30pm Saturday 7th November”.
4. Confirm Step 2 choice buttons sit at the bottom of all three travel cards.
5. Confirm Step 3 and the Tito selector are dark.
6. Confirm each ticket retains its visible ticket name.
7. Confirm the - / quantity / + controls form one right-aligned cluster.
8. Set Super Saver to its available limit and add one more of a ticket type. Confirm the extra ticket appears in the matching Advance type without leaving Super Saver above its limit. Repeat Advance -> Standard.
9. Confirm after automatic correction Tito's Continue button becomes usable when the selection is valid.
10. Confirm Standard availability follows remaining # Event Capacity. Issue/hold a comp or pre-school test admission and verify Standard remaining falls accordingly.
11. Confirm paid + Blue Badge parking share one 150-space counter.
12. Confirm “A valid Blue Badge must be displayed in the vehicle.” is within the Blue Badge ticket description.
13. Confirm no rendered ticket description says under-16s must be accompanied.
14. Complete a test checkout and confirm the post-purchase Tito email reminder still appears.

No new Netlify environment variables are required.
