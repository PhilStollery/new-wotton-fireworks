Wotton Fireworks 2026 Tito integration package v22
===================================================

PURPOSE
-------
V22 is cumulative over v21. It retains the v21 ticket counting, Standard reveal,
parking availability, load protection, metadata and security fixes, and applies
the latest customer-facing wording changes.

----------------

DO NOT REPLACE
--------------

That file contains the Tito operational IDs/slugs and may already have been
edited separately.

NO NETLIFY UI CHANGES ARE REQUIRED
----------------------------------
The existing TITO_API_TOKEN_TEST and TITO_API_TOKEN_LIVE variables remain the
only secrets.

V22 WORDING CHANGES
-------------------
1. PRE-SCHOOL
   - The static reminder now says:
     “Children who have not yet started Reception attend free, but please make
     sure they have a ticket.”
   - The FAQ and ticket terms use matching wording.

2. DRIVING / PARKING
   - The travel-planning card is now titled “Driving”.
   - It tells customers that if they plan to drive they must make sure parking
     has actually been booked in the ticket selector below.
   - It warns that Wotton Road will be closed to non-ticket holders and they
     will not be able to park there.
   - It states there is no event access from New Road and tells customers not to
     use New Road for parking.
   - The valid Blue Badge display requirement is retained.

3. TRAVEL PLANNING STATUS
   - The existing note remains: the travel choice is only to help plan likely
     demand, does not commit the customer to a travel method or reserve anything,
     and does not need updating if plans change later.

RETAINED FROM V21
-----------------
- Activity-based Super Saver and Advance counting.
- Standard reveal when the discounted allocations are exhausted.
- Non-numeric pre-school header.
- General/Blue Badge parking handling and sold-out messaging.
- Real customer-facing releases in Tito test mode.
- 30-second visible-tab availability refresh and server/CDN cache protection.
- Mandatory order-reference verification and rate limiting on response writes.
- `wfd_responses` metadata including likely travel method.
- Post-purchase warning that ticket emails come from Tito.
- Removal of the under-16 accompaniment restriction.

----------------------
1. Confirm the pre-school header contains no capacity figure and uses the new
   “make sure they have a ticket” wording.
2. Confirm the three travel cards read Walk / Bus / Driving.
3. Click Driving anywhere on the card and confirm it highlights.
4. Confirm the Driving card says parking must be booked below and includes the
   Wotton Road / New Road warnings.
5. Confirm the separate planning note says this is only demand-planning data and
   customers do not need to report later changes of plan.
6. Confirm Blue Badge parking still says a valid Blue Badge must be displayed.
7. Repeat the v21 checks for Advance counting, Standard reveal and parking
   availability.
8. Complete a test purchase and confirm checkout/payment behaviour is unchanged.
