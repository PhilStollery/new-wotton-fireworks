Wotton Fireworks 2026 Tito integration package v20
===================================================

PURPOSE
-------
V20 is cumulative over v19 and fixes the five issues found in preview testing.
It keeps the established Tito checkout flow intact.

DO NOT REPLACE
--------------

That file contains the Tito operational IDs/slugs and may already have been
edited separately. V20 reads it if populated, but preview mode can also identify
the on-sale customer releases by their Super Saver / Advance / Standard /
pre-school / parking names.

NO NETLIFY UI CHANGES ARE REQUIRED
----------------------------------
The existing TITO_API_TOKEN_TEST and TITO_API_TOKEN_LIVE variables remain the
only secrets.

V20 FIXES
---------
1. TRAVEL CARDS
   - Walk and Parking are now whole-card selections (mouse and keyboard).
   - Bus offers separate Wotton and Charfield choices.
   - Paid and Blue Badge parking are combined into one Parking travel card.
   - The Parking card explicitly says the selection only records travel intent;
     the customer must still select the number and type of parking spaces below.

2. ADMISSION COUNTER WORDING
   - Super Saver / Advance / Standard counters are explicitly treated as
     admission and cannot show “parking options below”.

3. PRE-SCHOOL
   - The pre-school ticket remains outside the paid waves.
   - Its header is always a static reminder that every attendee needs a ticket.
   - Overall event-capacity numbers are never shown against the pre-school ticket.

4. BLUE BADGE
   - Travel, FAQ/visit copy and the Blue Badge ticket header state that a valid
     Blue Badge must be displayed in the vehicle.

5. TEST MODE RELEASES
   - Preview/test mode now mounts the real customer-facing releases that are on
     sale in Tito, instead of the old Test Wave ticket releases.
   - Super Saver and Advance use the advertised 300 / 700 preview allocations;
     Standard is the final band and is not given a fake public capacity counter.
   - Pre-school, paid parking and Blue Badge parking are mounted alongside them.
   - The Tito widget still loads its test_mode plugin, so preview purchases remain
     test transactions.

RETAINED FROM V19
-----------------
- Netlify/Tito availability caching and 30-second visible-tab refresh.
- Mandatory order-reference verification and rate limiting on response writes.
- wfd_responses metadata including travel preference.
- Post-purchase warning that confirmation/ticket emails come from Tito.
- Removal of the under-16 accompaniment restriction.

TEST ON DEPLOY PREVIEW
----------------------
1. Confirm the travel area has three cards: Walk, Bus and Parking.
2. Click Walk and Parking anywhere on the card and confirm each visibly selects.
3. Confirm Bus from Wotton and Bus from Charfield are independently selectable.
4. Confirm Parking says that it does not reserve a space and that the number/type
   of parking spaces must still be selected in the ticket list below.
5. Confirm the ticket widget shows the real Super Saver ticket types in Tito's
   ascending-age order, with Advance and Standard hidden until needed.
6. Confirm the pre-school ticket is first and its header contains no capacity
   number and no “parking options” wording.
7. Confirm paid parking and Blue Badge parking appear after admission tickets.
8. Confirm a valid Blue Badge requirement is visible with Blue Badge parking.
9. Temporarily exhaust the general parking test allocation (or use a test release
   state) and confirm the page clearly says general parking is sold out and
   directs customers to walk or use the Wotton/Charfield buses.
10. Complete a test purchase and confirm Tito checkout/payment remains unchanged.
11. Confirm the post-purchase panel warns that confirmation/ticket emails come
    from Tito.
12. Inspect Tito registration metadata and confirm wfd_responses.travel is saved.

IMPORTANT
---------
If preview still cannot identify one of the real price bands, check the release
names/slugs. V20 first uses any live release slugs already entered in
integration-config-2026.mjs; otherwise it recognises releases containing
“Super Saver”, “Advance” and “Standard” in their title/slug.
