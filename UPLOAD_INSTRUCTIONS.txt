Wotton Fireworks 2026 Tito integration package v13
===================================================

V13 POST-CHECKOUT FOLLOW-UP
---------------------------
This version keeps the now-working Tito checkout handoff and adds the post-checkout journey.

After a successful order:
1. Tito's completed-order panel remains visible.
2. When it is dismissed, the ticket selector is replaced by the Wotton follow-up panel.
3. The Round Table invitation is prominent:
   - climbing / bouldering interest;
   - Friday 13 November 2026, 8pm meet-and-greet at Beermongery.Inc,
     40 Long St, Wotton-under-Edge, with Round Table, Ladies Circle,
     41 Club and Tangent.
4. Three separate quick choices follow:
   - cancellation donation/refund preference;
   - 2027 fireworks notification consent;
   - other Round Table/community event consent.
5. Each answer saves immediately against the completed Tito registration.
6. The customer can then Plan your visit or Book more tickets.

IMPORTANT
---------
The climbing and meet-and-greet responses are stored as interest/RSVP metadata
against the fireworks booking. They do not create a second Tito ticket.
