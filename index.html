What's on

One evening. Two displays.

The night builds from the first quieter display through live entertainment and the bonfire to the main high-level firework display.

    Book for Wotton-under-Edge

    Tickets go on sale at 5pm on Monday 21 September. The first 1,000 paid admission tickets are discounted, so booking early gets the best price.

    How are you getting here?

    If you need parking, select it with your admission booking. Everyone enters through the Wotton Community Sports Centre entrance.

    Paid parking is separate from admission; Blue Badge parking is free but still needs to be reserved.
